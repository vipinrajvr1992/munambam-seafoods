Our Heritage & Story

About Munambam Seafoods

Welcome to Munambam Seafoods. We are a dedicated coastal enterprise rooted deeply in Kerala's rich fishing heritage, specializing in premium, hygienically dried fish, prawns, and authentic homemade seafood delicacies.

Our journey is built on a simple vision: to bridge the gap between traditional coastal fishing communities and modern households seeking pure, chemical-free, and nutrient-rich seafood provisions.

From Muziris to Munambam Harbour

Munambam Seafoods is a continuation of the maritime glory that defined this coast for millennia. Located near the ancient port of Muziris —the legendary gateway that connected Kerala to the Roman Empire and the global spice and seafood trade—we operate on the very same shores where seafaring and commerce shaped civilizations.

Today, Munambam Harbour stands as the vibrant heartbeat of Kerala’s modern fishing industry. We draw direct inspiration from this rich history, blending the time-tested wisdom of coastal preservation with modern craftsmanship.

Our Core Commitments

  • Direct Harbour Sourcing: Every single batch is sourced directly from Munambam Harbour to guarantee peak freshness and authenticity.
  • 100% Chemical-Free: We strictly avoid synthetic preservatives, heavy salts, and harmful chemical additives during processing.
  • Traditional Hygiene: Our products undergo careful artisanal drying methods combined with modern hygienic packaging standards.
  • Small-Batch Excellence: To preserve true flavors and nutritional value, everything is prepared and packed in limited, quality-controlled batches.

Our Mission

To deliver the pristine, authentic taste of Kerala’s coastal heritage directly to your kitchen while empowering local fishing communities and ensuring uncompromising quality in every single pack.
