Our Story & Vision

About Munambam Seafoods

Munambam Seafoods was born from a shared vision between two people who believe that seafood from Kerala deserves a strong place in the premium global market.

Founded by Vipinraj V R and Krishna Prasoon N P, Munambam Seafoods brings together two complementary strengths — fish processing, design, technology and digital marketing on one side, and aquaculture knowledge, field experience, marketing and communication on the other.

Our connection to seafood is not simply a business decision. We are based close to Kodungallur and the historic Muziris region, surrounded by the coastal environment, fishing communities and harbour ecosystem that have been part of this region's identity for generations.

Being close to the coast gives us a direct connection to the world behind the product — the people, the fishing environment, the seafood and the realities of handling and processing it.

That connection is at the heart of Munambam Seafoods.

The People Behind Munambam

Vipinraj V R

Co-Founder · Fish Processing · Design · Technology · Digital Marketing

Vipinraj brings together a background in Fish Processing Technology through VHSE with skills in design, web development and digital marketing.

His role extends beyond creating the visual identity of Munambam Seafoods. He focuses on building the brand's digital presence, communication, technology, customer experience and overall brand presentation.

His vision is to help build Munambam Seafoods as a modern, technology-driven seafood brand with an identity strong enough to represent Kerala on an international platform.

Krishna Prasoon N P

Co-Founder · Aquaculture Professional · Marketing & Communications

Krishna Prasoon brings professional knowledge and practical experience from the aquaculture field, supported by an Aquaculture degree and postgraduate qualification.

Alongside his technical background, he has strong capabilities in marketing, communication and relationship building, with an ability to understand people, markets and changing customer needs.

His interest in continuous learning and adaptability allows him to keep developing his knowledge across different areas of the seafood and business ecosystem.

His combination of aquaculture knowledge, practical experience, marketing understanding and communication skills provides Munambam Seafoods with an important connection between product knowledge and market requirements.

Two Different Strengths. One Common Goal.

The foundation of Munambam Seafoods comes from the combination of our individual strengths.

Vipinraj brings fish processing knowledge, design, technology and digital marketing.

Prasoon brings aquaculture knowledge, practical industry experience, marketing, communication and a strong understanding of the seafood ecosystem.

Together, these complementary skills allow us to look at seafood from different perspectives — from the product itself to the people who produce it, the market that receives it and the brand that represents it.

This is not simply about selling seafood.

It is about building something we can stand behind.

Why We Started

We believe seafood can be much more than a commodity.

It can be clean, carefully handled, thoughtfully processed, nutritious and presented to a premium standard.

Our goal is to develop seafood products with a clear philosophy:

  • Natural.
  • Hygienic.
  • Protein-rich.
  • Mindful of sodium.
  • Free from unwanted chemical additives.
  • Uncompromising on quality.

We are not interested in building just another seafood label. We want to build a trusted seafood brand with a clear identity, strong standards and a genuine connection to its origin.

Every decision we make begins with one question:

Would we confidently put our own names behind this product?

If the answer is not yes, it does not belong under the Munambam name.

Rooted in Munambam

Our location is an important part of who we are.

Living close to Kodungallur and Muziris means being close to a region deeply connected with Kerala's maritime history and coastal life.

The harbour, fishing communities and coastal environment are not distant concepts to us.

They are part of the place we call home.

This proximity gives us an opportunity to understand seafood closer to its source and to build a brand that carries a genuine coastal identity rather than simply using one as a marketing story.

Munambam is not just our brand name. It represents where our story begins.

From the Coast to the World

Our roots are local.

Our ambition is global.

Munambam Seafoods is being built with an international outlook from the very beginning. Our long-term vision is to develop the brand and its products to meet the expectations of customers beyond India, with a focus on opportunities in markets such as the Middle East and Europe, while building the quality systems, processes and standards required for international trade and export.

We want to take products inspired by Kerala's coast and present them to the world through a brand that feels modern, trustworthy and premium.

Our ambition is not simply to export a product.

It is to export the identity, quality and character of Munambam.

Quality Without Compromise

For us, premium does not simply mean beautiful packaging or a higher price.

Premium means what is inside the package, how the product is handled, how it is processed, how it is packed and whether we can confidently put our own names behind it.

That is why quality is not an additional feature of Munambam Seafoods.

Quality is the foundation of the brand.

We believe that every product carrying the Munambam name should reflect our commitment to:

  • Careful sourcing.
  • Hygienic handling.
  • Responsible processing.
  • Natural product quality.
  • Thoughtful packaging.
  • Consistent standards.

And above all:

No compromise on quality.

Our Vision

To build Munambam Seafoods into a trusted premium seafood brand from Kerala with an international presence.

A brand where coastal tradition meets modern technology, aquaculture knowledge meets market understanding, and local identity meets global ambition.

And where every product carries the confidence of the people who created it.

Our Promise

We are building Munambam Seafoods one product, one customer and one standard at a time.

We may have started close to Munambam Harbour, but our vision reaches far beyond the coast.

We want customers to know where their seafood comes from, understand the people behind the brand, and feel confident about the quality they receive.

Because ultimately, a strong brand is not built only by products.

It is built by trust.

And that trust is something we intend to earn — one pack at a time.
