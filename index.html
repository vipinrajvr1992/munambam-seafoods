Frequently Asked Questions

How is Munambam Dried Seafood different from regular market dried fish?

Regular market dried fish is often dried on open sand, heavily salted to mask age, or treated with chemical sprays to deter insects. Munambam Seafoods is processed on elevated solar mesh beds, cleaned with purified water, and completely chemical-free.

How should I store the vacuum-sealed dried seafood?

Our vacuum packs keep the seafood fresh at room temperature in a cool, dry place for months. For extended freshness up to a year, you can refrigerate or freeze the sealed pack without any odor transfer.

Do I need to soak or wash the dried fish before cooking?

We recommend a quick 5 to 10-minute soak in plain water to remove excess natural curing sea salt, followed by a light rinse before cooking in your favorite curries or fries.
