The Munambam Maritime Heritage & History

Munambam Harbour, located at the northern tip of Vypeen Island in Kochi, Kerala, stands as one of the state's largest and most economically vibrant fishing harbors. Situated at the confluence where the Periyar River empties into the Arabian Sea, Munambam has been the beating heart of Kerala’s coastal trade and deep-sea fishing industry for decades.

Historically, coastal communities here mastered the art of sun-curing and traditional fish processing—a vital method evolved to preserve rich seafood harvests without refrigeration. At Munambam Seafoods, we honor this deep-rooted heritage by combining age-old artisanal expertise with modern hygienic processing protocols, ensuring that every packet of dried fish and prawn we deliver carries the authentic taste of the Arabian Sea.
