About Munambam Seafoods

From Munambam Harbour to the world — built on coastal roots, technical knowledge, modern technology, and a commitment to quality.

Our Story & Vision

Munambam Seafoods was born from a shared vision between two founders who believe that seafood from Kerala deserves a strong, recognized place in the premium global market.

Founded by Vipinraj V R and Krishna Prasoon N P, Munambam Seafoods brings together two complementary strengths — fish processing technology, digital product design, technology, marketing, and communications on one side, and industrial fisheries expertise, practical field experience, business development, and market strategy on the other.

Our connection to seafood is not simply a business decision. We are based close to Kodungallur and the historic Muziris region, deeply connected with the coastal environment, fishing communities, and harbour ecosystem that have been part of this region’s identity for generations.

Being close to the coast gives us a direct connection to the world behind the product — the people, the fishing environment, the seafood, and the realities of handling and processing it.

That connection is at the heart of Munambam Seafoods.

Why We Started

We believe seafood should be much more than a generic commodity. It can be clean, carefully handled, thoughtfully processed, nutritious, and presented to a premium standard.

Our product philosophy rests on six non-negotiable principles:

  1. Natural: 100% pure coastal produce with no artificial fillers.
  2. Hygienic: Processed under strict sanitation and clean environmental standards.
  3. Protein-Rich: Nutrient-dense seafood sourced straight from prime fishing grounds.
  4. Mindful of Sodium: Balanced and controlled salt levels tailored for healthy consumption.
  5. Chemical-Free: Zero harmful preservatives or chemical treatments.
  6. Uncompromising Quality: Consistent high standards maintained across every single batch.

We are not building a high-volume label; we are building a trusted brand anchored in transparency, origin, and standard.

"Would we confidently put our own names behind this product?"

If the answer is not an absolute yes, it does not carry the Munambam name.

Rooted in Munambam

Living close to Kodungallur and Muziris places us at the epicenter of Kerala’s maritime legacy.

The harbour, the fishing fleets, and the coastal communities are not marketing concepts to us — they form our local environment. This proximity enables direct oversight at the harbour level, allowing us to build a brand rooted in genuine coastal authenticity.

Munambam is more than our name; it is where our origin and accountability reside.

From the Coast to the World

While our roots are local, our outlook is global.

Munambam Seafoods is engineered with an international orientation from day one. Our long-term vision focuses on meeting global market standards, targeting opportunities across the Middle East (GCC) and Europe, and establishing export-compliant quality systems.

We aim to take Kerala’s coastal heritage and deliver it globally through a modern, reliable, and premium brand.

Our objective is not merely exporting a commodity — it is exporting the identity, quality, and trust of Munambam.
