Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Munambam Seafoods respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal data responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and how you can contact us about your data.

1. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect only the information reasonably required for the relevant purpose, including:

  • Identity and contact details: name, mobile number, email address and delivery address.
  • Order information: products ordered, quantities, delivery details, order status and relevant transaction records.
  • Customer communications: messages, enquiries, complaints and other information you choose to provide when contacting us.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser and website interaction information where required for website security, functionality and performance.

2. Why We Process Your Personal Data

We process personal data only for specific and legitimate purposes connected with our business, including:

  • Receiving, confirming, preparing and delivering seafood orders.
  • Communicating with you about orders, delivery, enquiries and customer support.
  • Processing or recording payments and maintaining appropriate business and transaction records.
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, security incidents and unauthorized activity.
  • Meeting applicable tax, accounting, consumer-protection and other legal obligations.
  • Sending promotional communications only where permitted and where the required consent or other lawful basis exists.

3. Notice, Consent & Your Choices

Where consent is required for processing, we will seek it in a clear manner for the relevant purpose. You may withdraw consent where applicable. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawfully carried out before withdrawal and may affect our ability to provide a service that depends on that processing.

4. Your Data Rights

Subject to applicable law and the relevant conditions, you may request information about the personal data we process, correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information, and erasure of personal data where retention is not required for a specified purpose or by law. You may also raise a grievance regarding our processing of your personal data.

5. How to Exercise Your Rights or Raise a Grievance

To request access, correction, updating, erasure, withdrawal of consent where applicable, or to raise a privacy grievance, contact us using the details below. Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant order or interaction and understand your request.

Email: connect.munambamseafoods@gmail.com

6. Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Where necessary to provide our services or meet legal obligations, relevant information may be shared with service providers such as delivery partners, payment or transaction service providers, website or hosting providers, communication platforms, professional advisers, or government and legal authorities where required.

Such sharing is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable law and appropriate contractual or security safeguards where applicable.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse and other security risks. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we also encourage customers to protect their own devices and account or communication credentials.

8. Retention & Erasure

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to complete an ongoing customer or business relationship, to maintain required records, to resolve disputes, or to comply with applicable legal obligations. When personal data is no longer required and there is no legal reason to retain it, we will take reasonable steps to erase or anonymize it.

9. Children

Our services are intended for customers who can lawfully provide personal data and place orders. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children for purposes that are not permitted under applicable law.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version published on this page will describe our current privacy practices.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, requests or grievances, contact Munambam Seafoods at connect.munambamseafoods@gmail.com or through our official WhatsApp contact at +91 79078 04747.
