PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Munambam Seafoods. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle information when you interact with us.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Munambam Seafoods respects the privacy of its customers, visitors, business partners, and other individuals who interact with our website or services. We aim to handle personal information responsibly, transparently, and only for legitimate business purposes.

By using our website or communicating with us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive information such as:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and other details you choose to provide.
  • Business information: company name, role, enquiries, requirements, and communication related to our products or services.
  • Website information: device, browser, general usage, and technical information that may be generated when you visit our website.
  • Communication records: information contained in messages, enquiries, feedback, or other communications you send to us.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries, requests, and customer communications.
  • Provide information about Munambam Seafoods, our products, and business activities.
  • Process and manage business relationships and legitimate commercial requests.
  • Improve our website, communication, customer experience, and internal operations.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and protect the integrity of our services.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.

Cookies & Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, understand website usage, improve performance, and provide a better user experience.

Where applicable, you can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

Data Sharing

Munambam Seafoods does not sell personal information as a business practice. Information may be shared with trusted service providers, professional advisers, technology providers, or relevant authorities where reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide services, protect our interests, or comply with applicable law.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, we seek to use appropriate safeguards and require information to be handled for legitimate purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or loss. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business requirements, or as required by applicable law.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or other appropriate action. Requests can be made through our official contact channels.

We may need to verify a request before taking action and may retain certain information where retention is legally or legitimately required.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Munambam Seafoods is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Children's Privacy

Our website and business services are not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services, or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with the revised policy taking effect from its stated or published date.

Contact Us

If you have a privacy-related question, request, or concern, please contact Munambam Seafoods through the contact details provided on our website.
